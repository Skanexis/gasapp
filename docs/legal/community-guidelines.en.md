# GasApp Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026. **Version:** 2026-11-12.
[Русская версия](community-guidelines.md).
If the translation and this English text differ, the English text
prevails.

GasApp exists so that people can talk privately and safely. Privacy is
not a licence to hurt others. These Guidelines apply to everything you do
in GasApp — private messages, channels, bots, usernames, channel names
and pictures — and form part of our [Terms of Service](terms-of-use.en.md).

## Prohibited content and behaviour

### Child safety — zero tolerance

We prohibit any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or
endangers children, including:

* child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including drawn, animated or
  AI-generated material;
* sexualising minors, or sexual conversations with someone you know or
  believe to be a minor;
* grooming, sextortion and trafficking;
* any attempt to contact a minor for these purposes.

Accounts and channels involved are deleted immediately, and apparent CSAM
is reported to the competent authorities. See our
[Child Safety Standards](../store/child-safety-standards.md).

### Violence and dangerous organisations

* Threats of violence against any person or group.
* Incitement to violence, or praise, support or recruitment for
  terrorist or violent extremist organisations and acts.
* Instructions for making weapons capable of mass harm.
* Content that encourages suicide or self-harm. If you or someone you know
  is in danger, contact local emergency services.

### Harassment, bullying and hate

* Targeted harassment, bullying, stalking or intimidation.
* Doxxing: sharing someone's private information (address, documents,
  phone number, photos) to expose or endanger them.
* Attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion,
  caste, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or
  serious illness.

### Sexual content

* Intimate images or videos of anyone shared without their consent,
  including threats to share them.
* Sexual content in channels whose name, picture or description does not
  make clear that it is intended for adults, and any sexual content
  involving minors (see above).

### Fraud, scams and impersonation

* Scams, phishing and fraudulent schemes, including fake investments,
  fake giveaways and requests for money under false pretences.
* Impersonating a person, a brand, an organisation or GasApp itself, or
  using a username or channel name designed to mislead.
* Asking users for their PIN, recovery key or confirmation codes. **GasApp
  will never ask you for them.**

### Illegal goods and services

The sale, promotion or facilitation of illegal drugs, weapons,
explosives, counterfeit goods or documents, stolen data or accounts,
hacking services, or any other goods and services that are illegal where
you or your audience are.

### Spam and platform abuse

* Bulk, repetitive or unsolicited messages and invitations.
* Bots that message people who did not contact them first, or that
  pretend to be humans.
* Automated account creation, buying or selling accounts and usernames,
  and username squatting for resale.
* Malware, attacks on our servers or users, attempts to bypass limits,
  and attempts to de-anonymise other users.

### Intellectual property

Publishing content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights.
Rights holders can send notices to `abuse@gasapp.eu`.

## Channels and bots

* A channel is public speech to everyone who has its link. Its **owner and
  administrators are responsible** for what is posted in it.
* A channel's name, `@name`, picture and description must follow these
  Guidelines too.
* A bot's owner is responsible for everything the bot sends and for whom
  it admits to a closed channel.

## How to protect yourself

* **Chat requests.** A first message from someone new arrives as a
  request. You can accept it or decline it; declining tells the sender
  nothing.
* **Block.** Contact menu → Block. Their messages are dropped on your
  device.
* **Report a person.** Contact menu → Report (or Report and block).
* **Report a channel.** Channel menu → Report channel, then choose a
  reason. You decide whether to give us access to the channel so that we
  can see what you are reporting.
* **Verify.** Compare the safety number with people you trust, in person
  or over video.
* **Leave a channel.** Channel menu → Leave.
* **By email.** `abuse@gasapp.eu`. Screenshots help: they are often the
  only way we can see reported content.

## What we can and cannot see

Private messages are end-to-end encrypted: we cannot read, scan or remove
them. What we act on is a report and the account or channel behind it. A
report about a private conversation proves cryptographically that the
reported account wrote to you; it does not send us the conversation.

Channels without a public `@name` are encrypted too: we see their content
only if a reporter chooses to share access. Channels with a public
`@name` can be read by the server.

## Enforcement

* We review reports **within 24 hours**; child safety reports first.
* Depending on how serious the violation is, we may dismiss the report,
  remove posts or a channel, revoke the right to create channels and
  bots, or suspend or delete an account. Severe violations lead to
  immediate deletion without warning.
* Where the law requires, or where someone's life or safety is at risk, we
  pass information to the competent authorities.
* **Appeals:** if you believe we made a mistake, write to
  `abuse@gasapp.eu` within six months. A person not involved in the
  original decision will review it.
