## 3. What GasApp is

* **Private messages** are end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read them, and
  we cannot recover or moderate their content.
* **Channels** are one-to-many feeds. A channel is **not** a private
  conversation: anyone who has its link can read it; a channel with a public
  `@name` can also be read by the server. A closed channel is joined by a
  request that whoever runs it approves.
* **Bots** are accounts run by a program of their owner. When you write to a
  bot, your message is read by that program and whoever runs it.
* **Premium** (the right to create channels and bots) is granted by the
  operator by hand. Nothing is sold in the app.
